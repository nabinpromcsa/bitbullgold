Built for disciplined operation

A trading workstation, not a black box.

Every mode exposes its state. Every live action remains behind explicit controls.

01

Market discovery

Track recent Solana candidates and evaluate liquidity, volume, transactions, price movement and buy-to-sell activity.

02

Selectable strategies

Choose strict safety, broad momentum or rapid five-minute momentum. Switching modes clears pending confirmations and disarms execution.

03

Position protection

Configure stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, daily loss limits, position limits and wallet reserve.

04

Wallet boundaries

Create an encrypted local wallet, verify its backup, choose an unlock duration and keep Phantom separate as an optional read-only connection.

Layered controls

Live trading is never a single switch.

BitBullGold starts in demo mode. Going live requires deliberate configuration, an unlocked verified wallet and a separate execution-arming step.

  1. 01

    Choose the runtime mode

    Demo mode blocks transaction signing and submission.

  2. 02

    Set risk boundaries

    Trade size, reserve, open positions, daily trades and daily loss are configurable.

  3. 03

    Verify and unlock the wallet

    Encrypted backup verification and timed wallet access are handled separately.

  4. 04

    Arm live execution

    Changing strategy or runtime mode disarms execution and resets pending confirmations.
